Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

🔒 Klipza processes everything on your device. Your videos, photos, and personal data never leave your iPhone.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data:

  • No account required — You do not need to create an account to use the App.
  • No video/photo upload — All editing, effects, AI processing, and rendering happen locally on your device.
  • Subscription status — Managed by Apple. We receive a purchase receipt but not your payment details.
  • Anonymous analytics — Basic app usage data (feature usage, crash reports) to improve the App. No personal information is included.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your videos, photos, or audio files
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Browsing history

3. On-Device Processing

All AI features — including background removal, auto captions, enhance, noise reduction, and object detection — run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks (Vision, Core ML, Metal). No data is sent to external servers for processing.

4. Music & Templates

When you browse or download music tracks and templates, the App connects to our content server to fetch catalog data and audio files. No personal information is sent — only the content request.

5. Advertising

The free version may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit — For subscription management
  • Google AdMob — For ads in the free version
  • GIPHY — For sticker search (queries are sent to GIPHY's API)

7. Data Retention

Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to retain or delete. Your projects and media stay on your device and in your iCloud (if enabled by you).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your Rights

You can delete the App at any time, which removes all local data. To cancel a subscription, go to Apple ID Settings → Subscriptions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@klipza.app.
