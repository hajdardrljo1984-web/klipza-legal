Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Rustfang Arena does not require an account. Game progress stays on your device. Ads (if you play for free) are provided by Google AdMob.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Rustfang Arena (the “App”), published by Hajdar Drljo (“we”, “us”). Bundle ID: com.hajdar.autoiphone.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run and improve the App:

  • No account required — You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email, or phone number to play.
  • Local game progress — Coins, unlocked cars/arenas, upgrades, missions, and settings are stored on your device (and, if you use Apple features that sync, may be handled by Apple).
  • In-app purchases — Managed by Apple StoreKit. We receive purchase status / receipts so we can unlock No Ads or the VIP Pack. We never see your payment card details.
  • Game Center (optional) — If you are signed into Game Center, scores and achievements may be submitted to Apple’s Game Center so leaderboards and achievements work. That data is processed under Apple’s terms.
  • Advertising data (free version) — If you have not purchased No Ads / VIP, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), rough device/app info, and ad interaction data to show ads and measure them. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Privacy / consent signals — In regions that require it (e.g. EEA/UK), we may show a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads.
  • Diagnostics — Basic crash or performance-related data may be collected to keep the App stable. This is not used to identify you personally.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number (unless you contact us yourself)
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera content
  • Browsing history outside the App

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide gameplay, save progress, and unlock purchases you bought
  • To show ads in the free version and keep the game free
  • To operate optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements
  • To fix bugs and improve performance
  • To comply with law (including advertising and privacy rules)

5. Tracking & App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, if the App asks for permission to track across apps and websites owned by other companies, that is only used to help deliver more relevant ads via AdMob. You can refuse. You can also reset or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings. Players who buy No Ads or the VIP Pack do not see ads.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple — App Store, StoreKit (purchases), Game Center (optional)
  • Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform — Ads and consent in the free version (privacy policy)
  • Unreal Engine (Epic Games) — Game engine technology that may process limited technical data as part of running the App

These providers process data under their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Retention

Local progress remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Purchase records are retained by Apple as needed to restore buys. Ad and consent data retained by Google is governed by Google’s policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is a combat driving game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such data, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not run user accounts, most App data is deleted by uninstalling the App. For ads and tracking, use iOS Settings and any in-app consent options. For purchase issues, use Apple’s App Store support / Restore Purchases in the App.

EEA/UK users can also manage ad consent via the consent form where shown, and may contact us at the email below.

10. International Transfers

Apple and Google may process data in the United States or other countries. Their safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable) apply to those transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
