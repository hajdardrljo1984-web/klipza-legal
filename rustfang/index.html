Rustfang Arena — Support

Help for the iPhone arena combat game by Hajdar Drljo.

Email support: hajdar.drljo@icloud.com

We usually reply within a few days. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug.

Restore purchases

Bought No Ads or VIP Pack, then reinstalled the game? Open the in-game shop and tap Restore Purchases. You must be signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Ads

The free version shows ads so the game can stay free. Buy No Ads or the VIP Pack to remove them permanently.
